📋 Supported Sequence Formats & File Types
1. Peptide List Mode
Supported Files: .pep, .txt or direct text paste.
Format: One peptide per line (e.g. AAAWYLWEV).
Length: 8–14 amino acids (canonical MHC-I length, 9-mer recommended). Single-letter IUPAC amino acid codes (A-Z).
2. FASTA Protein Sequence Mode
Supported Files: .fasta, .fsa, .fa, .txt or direct text paste.
Format: Header line starting with > (e.g. >sp|P0DTC2|SPIKE_SARS2), followed by sequence lines.
Sliding Window: Digested automatically based on Peptide lengths (e.g. 8,9,10) for epitope inference.

📖 ImmunoStruct Dual-Layer Multimodal Architecture

This platform implements the dual-layer multimodal prioritization architecture detailed in immunStruct.md based on Nature Machine Intelligence (2026). It combines sequence-based predictors with ImmunoStruct 3D Graph Neural Networks to discover hidden epitopes that sequence-only models miss.

🧬 Dual-Layer Immunogenicity Mechanism
Layer 4A: IEDB Sequence Immunogenicity   ∥   Layer 4B: ImmunoStruct 3D Graph Immunogenicity

Evaluating immunogenicity in parallel provides dual-layer evidence. ImmunoStruct processes pMHC 3D graph representations and TCR-facing bulky residue exposure, identifying Structure-Driven Candidates that sequence-only models penalize.

📊 6-Dimension Multimodal Composite Score Equation
Score = 0.30×Presentation + 0.10×Processing + 0.15×IEDB_Seq + 0.25×ImmunoStruct_3D + 0.10×HLA_Breadth + 0.10×Population
🌟 Double Validated (High IEDB + High ImmunoStruct)

Highest confidence golden candidates supported by both sequence immunogenicity and 3D graph features.

💡 Structure-Driven (Low IEDB + High ImmunoStruct)

Novel epitopes discovered exclusively through 3D pMHC structural protrusion and TCR interaction geometry!
