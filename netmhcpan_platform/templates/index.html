📋 Supported Sequence Formats & File Types
1. Peptide List Mode
Supported Files: .pep, .txt or direct text paste.
Format: One peptide per line (e.g. AAAWYLWEV).
Length: 8–14 amino acids (canonical MHC-I length, 9-mer recommended). Single-letter IUPAC amino acid codes (A-Z).
2. FASTA Protein Sequence Mode
Supported Files: .fasta, .fsa, .fa, .txt or direct text paste.
Format: Header line starting with > (e.g. >sp|P0DTC2|SPIKE_SARS2), followed by sequence lines.
Sliding Window: Digested automatically based on Peptide lengths (e.g. 8,9,10) for epitope inference.

ℹ️ Prediction Methods & Literature Sources
🧬 T-Cell Immunogenicity Score
Method: IEDB Class I T-Cell Immunogenicity Model (Position-weighted amino acid scale & anchor position masking)
Reference: Calis JJ, et al. "Properties of MHC Class I Presented Peptides That Inspire Immunogenicity." PLoS Comput Biol 9(10): e1003266 (2013).
Description: Score > 0 indicates peptide amino acid properties exposed to TCR are likely to trigger CD8+ T-cell immune response. Higher scores indicate stronger activation potential.
🎯 MHC Class I Presentation (%Rank EL)
Method: MHC-I Eluted Ligand (EL) Artificial Neural Network (Reynisson et al. Model)
Reference: Reynisson B, et al. Nucleic Acids Res 48(W1): W449–W454 (2020).
Description: %Rank ≤ 0.5% indicates Strong Binder (SB), %Rank ≤ 2.0% indicates Weak Binder (WB).
💊 Binding Affinity (Affinity_nM / IC50)
Method: Quantitative Peptide-MHC Binding Affinity Model (IC50 in nM)
Description: IC50 < 50 nM indicates high affinity; < 500 nM indicates moderate affinity.

📊 Output Metrics & Decision Cutoffs Guide

Standardized thresholds applied by the TRIAD engine to classify CD8+ T-cell epitope candidates:

Metric Column Full Name & Description Recommended Decision Cutoffs Biological Significance
%Rank_EL Eluted Ligand Percentile Rank ≤ 0.5% : Strong Binder (SB)
≤ 2.0% : Weak Binder (WB) 		Primary metric for natural cell-surface presentation likelihood.
Affinity_nM Binding Affinity (IC50 in nM) < 50 nM : High affinity
< 500 nM : Moderate affinity 		Thermodynamic stability of peptide-MHC binding groove interaction.
Immunogenicity_Score Physicochemical T-Cell Score > 0.0 (Positive log-odds) Intrinsic probability of TCR engagement based on position-weighted aromatic/hydrophobic residues (Calis et al. 2013).
Priority_Score Composite Priority Score (CPS) Normalized [0.0, 1.0] (Higher is better) Synthesizes presentation likelihood, TCR immunogenicity, and population coverage for candidate ranking.
🎯 Composite Score (Composed Index) & Tier Decision Methodology

To prevent single-metric biases (such as selecting peptides that bind strongly but lack TCR contact features, or vice versa), TRIAD automatically computes a unified Composite Score (0.0000 ~ 1.0000) and assigns Clinical/Experimental Decision Tiers (Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3) for every predicted peptide:HLA pair.

🧮 4-Dimensional Normalized Weighting Formula:
Composite_Score = 0.40 × S_pres + 0.25 × S_aff + 0.25 × S_imm + 0.10 × S_breadth
• S_pres (40%): Presentation likelihood derived from %Rank_EL (≤0.5% maps to 0.75~1.0)
• S_aff (25%): Log-transformed IC50 binding affinity (1 - log10(IC50)/log10(50000))
• S_imm (25%): Calis et al. TCR contact physicochemical propensity ((Score + 0.4)/0.8)
• S_breadth (10%): Cross-HLA presentation ratio across all tested target alleles
🌟 Tier 1 (High Priority)
Criteria: %Rank_EL ≤ 0.5% (Strong Binder) AND Immunogenicity > 0 AND Composite Score ≥ 0.65
Interpretation: Top-priority validation targets. Confirmed high-probability native presentation combined with enriched aromatic/hydrophobic TCR contact residues for CD8+ T-cell activation.
⚡ Tier 2 (Secondary)
Criteria: %Rank_EL ≤ 2.0% (Weak Binder) AND Composite Score ≥ 0.45
Interpretation: Secondary candidate pool. Displays competent presentation propensity and moderate binding affinity; ideal for expanding epitope screening libraries.
🌐 Tier 3 (Low Priority)
Criteria: %Rank_EL > 2.0% OR lacks positive immunogenicity propensity
Interpretation: Low activation probability or borderline presentation; suitable as negative controls or backup candidates.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q: Why are peptides constrained to 8–14 amino acids?
MHC Class I molecules possess closed peptide-binding grooves at both ends, restricting naturally presented epitopes to 8–14 residues (predominantly 9-mers). For intact proteins, switch to FASTA Format to allow the engine to automatically slide and digest into valid k-mers.
Q: How does the in-memory RAM Disk acceleration work?
All intermediate worker chunking and temporary IO pipelines write directly to Linux shared memory (/dev/shm/netmhc_tmp). This provides sub-millisecond IO throughput and automatically purges temporary data upon completion, ensuring zero physical SSD/HDD wear.
Q: Can I use patient-specific HLA typing?
Yes. You can search any HLA-A, HLA-B, or HLA-C 4-digit allele in the search box, or upload a custom text/CSV file containing your patient's typing under Regional Presets.
