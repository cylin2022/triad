Figure S2. In vitro binding to H2-Db and H2-Kb of in silico predicted neoantigens.
In silico predicted 10-mers derived from LLC1 cell line (Fig. 1a) were synthesized and tested for
their ability to bind to H2-Db and H-2Kb using an ELISA assay. Re- folding of the α-chain and the
β2 microglobulin-biotinylated light chain following peptide binding was monitored through
optical density (O.D.) measurement at 245 nm. Data are reported as mean±SEM. Ova257-264
SIINFEKL, Gp10025-33 KVPRNQDWL and Almsl LYLDSKSDTTV were used as positive
controls (+) for H2-Kb, H2-Db and H2-Kd, respectively. PBS was used as a negative control (-);
the black line represents the threshold for positive binding (O.D. of the negative control). Only
peptides with an O.D. above the threshold were selected for further in vivo study. Non-binding
peptides are highlighted in red. Related to Figure 1.

Figure S3. Immunogenicity of LLC1 neoantigens in vivo.
2x105 splenic CD8+ T cells obtained one week after immunization with DCs pulsed with candidate
neoantigens were re-challenged with the indicated recombinant peptides (1µg/mL) and analyzed
by IFN-γ ELISpot. Data show individual mice together with mean±SEM. Positivity (black line) is
defined as the mean IFN-γ production of the negative control peptide from another cell line (ID8
Tmem260 LHYLHYCEL) + 3SDs). PMA and ionomycin were used as positive controls. Related
to Figure 1.
